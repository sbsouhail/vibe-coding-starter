# Stuck on what to build? Start here

The challenge: an AI-powered service that brings **real added value** to real people.
Pick an idea, then make it yours: change the users, the problem or the twist.

| # | Idea | Who it helps | What the AI does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | StudyBuddy | Students | Turns course notes into quizzes with explanations |
| 2 | Campus FAQ assistant | New students | Answers questions about schedules, procedures and contacts |
| 3 | Internship CV coach | Students looking for internships | Reviews a CV or cover letter and suggests improvements |
| 4 | Derja helper | Anyone writing in Tunisian Arabic | Understands Derja or mixed French-Arabic and replies clearly |
| 5 | Small-shop assistant | Local shop owners | Writes product descriptions and replies to customer messages |
| 6 | Admin paperwork guide | Citizens | Explains which documents a procedure needs, step by step |
| 7 | Study planner | Students before exams | Builds a revision plan from the exam calendar and topics |
| 8 | Recipe from my fridge | Students living alone | Suggests meals from the ingredients you have, on a budget |
| 9 | Accessibility reader | People with low vision | Reads and summarises a photo of a document out loud |
| 10 | Event summariser | Clubs and associations | Turns meeting notes into a summary and a to-do list |

## Check your idea in one minute

- **Who** exactly uses it, and would they really use it?
- **What** problem does it solve that isn't solved today?
- **Where** is the AI essential, not just decoration?
- **Can** your team build a working version before the challenge deadline?
